How it works

  1. Read the clue above. It always describes one of the six buildings.
  2. Press Start the hunt on the map. The clue follows you to the campus map.
  3. Tap the building you think matches. A wrong guess just wobbles. A right guess reveals the relic and the next clue.
  4. Find all six to reveal the hidden verse and earn the Bell Tower stamp.

Stuck? The Hint button on the map gives a nudge. Progress is saved, so you can pause and come back.
