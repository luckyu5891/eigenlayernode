    Common questions

    What should I wear?

    Whatever you are comfortable in. You will see jeans, dresses, and everything in between.

    Will I be asked to give money?

    No. Giving is for people who call this church home. Guests are our guests.

    Is there something for my kids?

    Yes. Safe, fun, age-appropriate programs run during every service, with a secure check-in.

    I am not sure what I believe. Is that okay?

    Completely. Questions are welcome here, and no one will put you on the spot.

    Is the building accessible?

    Step-free entrances, accessible restrooms, and reserved parking are available at every building.
