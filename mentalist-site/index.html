What this is

Not a magician. Nothing is in his hands.

He works with what your guests are thinking: the name they didn’t say, the number they wrote down and folded away, the memory they have never told anyone.

In a boardroom of twelve or a ballroom of twelve hundred it lands the same way. Silence first. Then the room comes apart.

You just felt a small version of it on a screen. In the room, it is not small.

Watch the room, not the trick

Ninety seconds of faces.

Reactions only. No reveals, no explanations. If you want to know what your CEO looks like when someone reads a number out of her head, this is it.
